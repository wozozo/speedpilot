# Privacy Policy for SpeedPilot

Last updated: January 2025

## Overview

SpeedPilot is a Chrome extension that enhances video playback control. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of any information processed by our extension.

## Data Collection and Usage

### What we collect:
- **User Preferences**: Your customized settings (keyboard shortcuts, speed increments, disabled sites) are stored locally using Chrome's storage API
- **No Personal Data**: We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, browsing history, or video watching habits

### How data is stored:
- All settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage API
- No data is sent to external servers
- No analytics or tracking codes are included

## Permissions

SpeedPilot requires the following permissions:

### `storage`
- Used to save your preferences and settings locally
- Data never leaves your device

### `<all_urls>` (Host Permission)
- Required to inject the video control functionality into web pages
- Only accesses video elements on the page
- Does not read or modify any other page content

## Third-Party Services

SpeedPilot does not use any third-party services, analytics, or external APIs.

## Data Security

- All data is stored locally on your device
- We do not have access to your data
- No network requests are made by the extension

## Children's Privacy

SpeedPilot does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
